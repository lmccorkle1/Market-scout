How scores work

30% business fundamentals · 25% valuation · 20% selloff setup · 15% balance-sheet strength · 10% catalysts. All five inputs are analyst-assessed from 0–10; the dashboard calculates a 0–100 research-priority score. No statistical probability of success is implied.

A severe price decline is not automatically an opportunity. Falling earnings, dilution, excessive debt, and permanent competitive damage can turn a “dip” into a value trap.
